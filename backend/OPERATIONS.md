## Identity

Staff tokens remain `/api/auth/login` tokens. Portal tokens only authenticate `/api/portal/*` and cannot access staff APIs. Retired CRM APIs are absent and return 404. Portal profiles have a primary key on customer_id and unique username; each profile has one contact_name/email/phone/company_address. Provision through staff-admin `POST /api/operations/customers`, optionally linking an existing retained customer_id. There is no multiple-contact domain.

`POST /api/portal/login {username,password}` → token; GET me, POST logout. GET catalog returns published product series with `price_rule` or null. GET orders and consumption are company-scoped.

Staff `POST /operations/customers/{cid}` edits company/contact_name/email/phone/company_address and the customer type/code without creating another contact or rewriting order snapshots. New retail order numbers use `RYYMMDDnnA`; wholesale uses the saved customer code plus the unique job name. Legacy order numbers remain unchanged. Portal catalog includes source_code/configuration_schema/orderable/scope. The schema lists key/labels/choices/required/type. Raw source price matrices and internal cost records are omitted from portal catalog, calculations and order responses. Template-only products are excluded from the public product endpoints. Ashwood is display-only until a template rule exists.

## Orders and rules

`POST /api/portal/price` and `/api/portal/orders` accept project_name, site_address, submission_key, installation_required and lines `{window_name, product_id, width_mm, height_mm, quantity, panel_count, options}`. Quantity counts repeated windows; panel_count counts panels per window. Legacy unconfigured products may be saved pending_review with total=null, but cannot be invoiced. Imported display-only/missing/invalid matrix configurations are rejected. No client unit_price, discount or surcharge is accepted.

Imported shutters: area rounded upward to 2dp, minimum 0.5m², area rate plus confirmed add-ons; area percentage charges apply to area charges, while per-item charges are added after percentage. The order minimum floors total billable shutter area at 1m². Other blinds: next-greater-or-equal width/drop matrix lookup. Apply staff discount, then round the line unit upward to 2dp, multiply by repeated-window quantity. Shutter installation is $50 per panel with a $150 order minimum when selected. GST retains source precision; currency display uses 2dp, and the 60% deposit rounds the precise total upward. Results include subtotal/installation/tax/total/deposit/rule_version and per-line prices. The provided ClearPVC installation alias mismatch remains an explicit import warning and blocks that installation quote.

Staff-only `POST orders/{id}/adjust {note,lines:[{line_id,discount,product_surcharge,installation_surcharge}]}` accepts a source-listed discount and a meaningful note before review, recalculates and records history. Reviewed projects cannot be adjusted through this endpoint. Price-rule imports never rewrite existing snapshots.

Admin `POST /api/operations/pricing/{product_id}` accepts PriceRule in operations.py. Rules require evidence of the confirmed sheet, area/minimum/rounding/dimension limits, currency/tax_basis and exact option choices. The supported formula is explicitly selected: round dimensions upward by dimension_step_mm, area in m² subject to minimum; `(area * rate + sum fixed) * (1 + sum percent / 100) + sum installation`, rounded per unit then multiplied by quantity. An empty charge dictionary is a visual-only choice. This formula is an available configuration, **not a claim that the customer's unprovided sheet uses it**. No rules are seeded. Submit snapshots configured prices; review can price a previously unpriced pending order. Reviewed snapshots never change with the catalog.

Staff GET orders; POST orders/{id}/review generates one 60% deposit invoice draft. POST deposit-received records independent manual confirmation with actor/time, stores `deposit_paid_at` and starts the lead-time clock: `eta` = that business date + 10 weeks (12 when a shaped cut-out is chosen), a calendar date the customer sees. POST supplier-order may omit `{reference}` and requires that deposit confirmation. Supplier Excel export requires a confirmed deposit and uses the macro-free supplier order sheet; it includes the customer-facing order number and configuration, with no internal record ID or costs. GET `orders/{id}/production-sheet` and `job-sheet` produce macro-free XLSX files from the accepted snapshot, with typed dimensions/quantities and full configuration details. Both require staff authentication.

POST transit `{order_ids,operation_key}` gives per-order success/failed/skipped. POST fulfilment `{order_id,kind:'arrival'|'delivery',operation_key,lines:[{line_id,quantity}]}` records an order-level action. Arrival is accepted only when the submitted quantities complete every outstanding line to its full ordered quantity; delivery (pickup) is accepted only after the whole order arrived and when the action completes every undelivered line; payment is recorded last and never blocks it. Order status runs `pending_review → accepted → supplier_ordered → in_transit → ready → complete` (stored `arrived`/`delivered` read back as `ready`/`complete`). Partial quantities are rejected. Repeat identical operation keys do not duplicate; altered bodies conflict. History keeps order-level events; arrival_status/delivery_status/payment_status remain separate. Balance invoices are created only when every line has arrived. Frontend batch selection can submit per-order arrival requests and display each outcome.

Shipment batches: POST `/operations/shipments {name?,order_ids,eta?,operation_key}` creates a uniquely named batch from placed shutter orders and puts them in transit; GET `/operations/shipments` lists batches with their orders, GET `/operations/shipments/candidates` lists orders no batch has taken. POST `/shipments/{id}/eta {eta}` overwrites the ETA of every in-transit order in the batch and records `eta_updated` in history. POST `/shipments/{id}/arrive` makes every order `ready`, queues the arrival notices and drafts one balance invoice per company (idempotent).

Internal shipment costs: POST `/operations/shipments` accepts only placed shutter orders. POST `/shipments/{id}/costs` records land/sea freight and optional actual duty; freight allocates by ordered shutter area. POST `/orders/{id}/purchase-cost` records factory USD, converted to NZD at 1.70; duty is estimated at 15% until a real shipment duty total is entered, then actual duty replaces the estimate and allocates by converted factory purchase amount. These fields are staff-only and excluded from portal and supplier exports.

## Invoice, email and rebate boundaries

Balance invoice lines show each order's balance with GST included (no deposit column), then any rebate, the amount due and the GST inside it. POST `/operations/invoices/manual {customer_id,items:[{description,amount}],operation_key,order_ids?}` creates a separate `manual` invoice for charges outside the order balance; amounts include GST, credits are negative lines, the total must be positive. Bank details are saved by an administrator with POST `/operations/settings {payment_details:{account_name,bank_name,account_number,reference_note}}` and printed on every invoice email and PDF; until saved the footer only asks the customer to contact VERA. Customers use GET `/portal/invoices` (sent invoices only, `payment_status` paid/unpaid, `unpaid_count`, `unpaid_total`) and GET `/portal/invoices/{id}/pdf`; drafts and other companies' invoices return 404.

GET invoices, GET notifications; POST invoices/{id}/send explicitly sends a stable text invoice to the sole contact. This is a workflow prototype, not a confirmed statutory invoice layout/PDF. Arrival saves an outbox event then automatically attempts the email, showing only that batch. Statuses: pending/sending/sent/not_configured/delivery_unknown. Repeated calls never resend a sent or ambiguous email. POST notifications/{id}/retry is available after not_configured is fixed; ambiguous SMTP outcomes require manual mailbox reconciliation. A process interrupted while sending leaves `sending`, requiring that same reconciliation. Never label these as successfully sent.

POST rebates imports a confirmed monthly credit result with customer_id, source_order_ids, month, amount, currency and evidence. A source order cannot be counted again. There is no invented monthly ladder or automatic accrual formula. GET rebates explicitly reports confirmed_monthly_rules_required.

POST settlements takes order_ids, operation_key, rules_evidence and billing_basis=`all_arrived_remaining_total`. It supports only fully arrived, deposit-confirmed projects of one company/currency/tax_basis. It deducts actual saved deposits and eligible prior-month confirmed rebate balances; allocations and invoice claims are atomic. POST `invoices/{id}/received {note}` records manual balance receipt once, with actor/time, and marks its projects paid. Deposits still use the project-specific confirmation. Rebate accrual remains paused; explicitly imported prior credits continue to offset balances. Automatic monthly tiers, expiry/cancellation/refund rules, invoice corrections and confirmed invoice layout remain dependent work.

GET `/portal/consumption` and staff `/operations/customers/{cid}/consumption` return orders/invoices/rebates and currency-separated summary: ordered, invoiced, manually confirmed received and outstanding_invoiced. Unpriced projects are counted separately. These are explicit operational definitions, not a guessed rebate basis. Dashboard real analytics and customer ratings stay deferred per v5.
